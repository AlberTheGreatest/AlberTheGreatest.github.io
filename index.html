About

Creative producer with ten years in commercial film and photography for luxury and tech brands.

I set the creative direction and build each visual world from the first concept to the final frame.

At Keyzar Jewelry I established the in-house photo and video function and defined the brand's visual language across campaign shoots, commercials and product films.

Generative AI has been at the core of my process since 2024. I direct AI models and agents to deliver premium work at a volume traditional production cannot reach.
